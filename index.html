Touch-Activated LED Circuit

Interactive 2D demonstration using a TTP223 capacitive touch sensor module and a 3 V supply. Tap either touch pad or use the button to simulate touching the sensor.

LED OFF

Parts list

  • TTP223 capacitive touch sensor module — 1
  • LED — 1 (red or green)
  • 220 Ω resistor — 1
  • 3 V supply (2 × AA batteries and holder) — 1
  • Breadboard and jumper wires

Wiring concept: battery + to TTP223 VCC; battery − to GND; sensor OUT to the LED/resistor path; return to GND. Check your specific module pinout before wiring. Some TTP223 boards output a logic signal and should drive the LED through a suitable transistor or logic input; do not assume the module can safely supply any LED current directly.
